# Privacy Policy

**SearchPoint**

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how **SearchPoint**, operated by **PT Artha Rajamas Mandiri** under the **Hyperjump** brand, collects, uses, and protects personal data.  
SearchPoint is designed with **privacy, security, and enterprise compliance** as core principles.

## 2. Information We Collect

SearchPoint may collect limited information necessary to operate the service, including:

- User identity information (name, email, user ID) provided by identity providers
- Authentication metadata (login timestamps, tenant ID)
- Application usage logs for security and performance monitoring

SearchPoint **does not** collect passwords.

## 3. Source of Data

Personal data is obtained from:

- Enterprise identity providers (e.g., Microsoft Entra ID)
- Organization-configured authentication systems

## 4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Authenticate users
- Enforce access permissions
- Secure and operate the application
- Monitor system performance and security incidents

## 5. Data Access and Permissions

SearchPoint respects organization-defined access controls. Users can only access data they are authorized to view. We do not access or use customer content outside service operations.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell or share personal data for marketing purposes.  
Data may be shared only:

- With authorized infrastructure providers
- When required by law or legal process

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect data, including:

- Secure authentication mechanisms
- Encrypted communication
- Access control enforcement

## 8. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary to:

- Provide the service
- Meet security, auditing, or legal requirements

Retention policies may be governed by the customer organization.

## 9. User Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have the right to:

- Access their personal data
- Request correction or deletion
- Raise concerns through their organization’s administrator

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date.
